COW HAWK

Privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

We don’t run accounts

Cow Hawk has no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics of ours. We don’t sell your data. There is no user account.

What stays on your device

  • Your concept / vocab lists and language text you enter
  • Which languages are enabled on each concept
  • Game preferences (language pair, board size if any)
  • Custom card-back images you choose (copied or referenced on device)

Delete the app and that local data goes with it.

Photos

Optional. If you set a custom card back, iOS asks for Photos access when you pick an image. You can decline and still play with the default card back. Selected images stay on your device; we don’t upload them.

What leaves the phone

v0.1 play does not require network. We don’t send your lists, scores, or card-back images to Fishnet Hue servers. If a future optional feature needs the network, we’ll say so here and in the app.

Kids

We don’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date on this page.
