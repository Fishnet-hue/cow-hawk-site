Cow Hawk is an offline-first bilingual vocab memory / concentration card game by Fishnet Hue. Build concept lists, pick a language pair (English, Japanese, French, Klingon to start), match cards, and optionally set custom card backs from Photos. Everything stays on your device. No accounts.
